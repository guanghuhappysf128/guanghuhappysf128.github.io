About Me

Background & Interests

I am a Teaching Specialist in the School of Computing and Information Systems at the University of Melbourne. I began teaching at the university in 2017 and took up my current appointment in July 2026. I lecture in AI planning and algorithms, and in 2025 I helped develop AI Planning for Autonomy for Melbourne Online.

My research focuses on epistemic planning, multi-agent planning, and forward search. In my PhD, I studied how an agent can plan around what others have seen and what they believe, even when those beliefs are mistaken.
